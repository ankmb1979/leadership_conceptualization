Leadership Conceptualization Profile

How do you conceptualize leadership?

People can understand leadership in different ways. Some emphasize who leaders are, whereas others focus more strongly on learned abilities, skills, behavior, relationships, or the broader leadership process. The questionnaire will help you examine which perspectives most strongly shape your current understanding of leadership.

About the activity

You will respond to 24 statements using a five-point scale ranging from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree. Your responses will generate scores for six leadership conceptualizations: trait, ability, skill, behavior, relationship, and process.

There are no correct or incorrect profiles. The purpose is to make your assumptions about leadership more visible so that you can examine how those assumptions may influence your leadership practice.

Educational use and attribution: This interactive activity is based on Peter G. Northouse's Conceptualizing Leadership Questionnaire from Introduction to Leadership: Concepts and Practice and is adapted for educational use. The questionnaire is also available through SAGE's student resources for Northouse's text. Results are intended for reflection and learning rather than diagnosis or formal assessment. Responses remain in the browser and are not transmitted or stored.
